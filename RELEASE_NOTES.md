**Install:** download `Negusify-0.2.0.ipa` and install it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) using your Apple ID (with a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days).

Negusify for iPhone 0.2.0, The GUI Update: a Speed panel in the player (speed, pitch, link speed to pitch, keep across songs, reverb); a full lyrics screen (tap Lyrics in the player) with the cover flowing behind, words lighting up as they're sung and lines gliding like Liner's; the FruitGlass theme (Settings → Themes).

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.15: same as 0.1.13 on iPhone (this release fixes Spotify playing inside the Windows app); keeps the iPhone app on the same version as the desktop app.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.14: same as 0.1.13 on iPhone (this release is the desktop app playing Spotify songs inside Negusify); keeps the iPhone app on the same version as the desktop app.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.13: statuses like Discord (Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb, Invisible): tap your status under your name in the side panel; friends show a dot on their picture in Messages and on their profile. Do Not Disturb stops message buzzes. In a chat: a bell to mute it, and messages sent in the same minute share one time (like WhatsApp). New messages buzz and show a banner while the app is open. Statuses need the updated Negusify server.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.12: Messages with your friends (side panel), with songs shared as cards (cover, title, artist, album, play) from the song menu → Send to a friend, and a Message button on profiles; Playlists now above Liked Songs in the side panel. Messages need the updated Negusify server.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.11: import your listening history from Spotify (Extended streaming history or account data) and YouTube Music (Google Takeout) in Settings → Import listening history; the plays count in History, Stats and the leaderboards. Plus your friend's leaderboard updates and "Live" on live works.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.9: Leaderboards (side panel) with the top 10 neguses by listening time, Show more, and ranks on every profile; Copy link and Share in the song menu (a Spotify song gives its Spotify link even while it plays from YouTube Music or SoundCloud); paste a Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud song link in Search (or tap the clipboard button) to get that song.

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.8: albums of downloaded songs open offline (songs not downloaded show greyed out), tapping the cover in the player opens the album as a full page, History stats by Today / This week / This month / This year, "Various Artists" for albums with more than 7 artists, and Linux PCs listed as Linux. Includes everything from 0.1.5–0.1.7.
