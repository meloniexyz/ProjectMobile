**Install / update:** download the `.ipa` below and install it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) using the same Apple ID as before (your data stays). With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days.

- **The queue works like Spotify.** Add to queue puts songs right after the current one, in the order you add them (the first one added plays first), ahead of the rest. The queue shows **Next in queue**, then **Next up**. Queued songs keep their place when shuffling and when you start another playlist.
- **Swipe right on a song to add it to the queue**, like Spotify: a queue icon shows behind the song and fills in once you have swiped far enough.
- **Side panel**, like the Windows sidebar: swipe from left to right on Home (or tap the logo) for your profile, Home, Search, Library, Local files, History, Liked Songs, playlists and Settings.
- **Better touch:** buttons react with a small press and a soft tap, and songs no longer flash when you just scroll past them.
- **Buttons on the player work straight away:** song options, Save to playlist and messages now show over the player and the queue instead of after you leave them.
- **One playing bar:** swiping back from Settings or a profile no longer stacks two playing bars on Home.
- **Home** greets you by name, differently each time and fitting the time of day, and shows your playlists right after Jump back in.
- **Settings → Check for updates** (it also tells you when you are up to date).

Same in ProjectM 1.3.4 on Windows.
