**Install:** download `Negusify-0.1.5.ipa` and install it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) using your Apple ID (with a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days).

Negusify for iPhone 0.1.5: everything from the shared branch (Stats like the desktop, Artists page, library pages, one History, classical works grouping, player that opens the album), accounts on the Negusify server, and "Negus since" on profiles.
