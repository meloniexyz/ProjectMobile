**Install / update:** download the `.ipa` below and install it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) using the same Apple ID as before (your data stays). With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days.

- **Profiles, like Discord.** Your ProjectM account now has a profile: a banner (your own picture, or a solid colour), your photo (or your first letter), display name, @username, About me and Member since. Pick two profile colours and the card runs from one to the other. Home → your picture → Edit profile, with a live preview.
- **@usernames.** New accounts pick an @username when signing up (checked as you type). Accounts made before ask you to pick one once.
- **Find people.** Search → **ProjectM** (next to the music platforms) finds people by @username or name and opens their profile.
- **Your email stays private.** It is never on your profile, only in the account settings as stars with Show.
- **Launch screen** shows the logo you picked, in your colour (no more old logo).

Same in ProjectM 1.3.0 on Windows.
