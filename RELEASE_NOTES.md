**Install / update:** download the `.ipa` below and install it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) using the same Apple ID as before (your data stays). With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days.

- **YouTube songs play again.** The app now asks YouTube the way its iPhone, YouTube Music and mobile-site apps do before the older TV way, and uses whichever works (remembered for next time). If YouTube still won't give a song, the same recording plays from SoundCloud.
- **The same version of a song on every device.** When a Spotify song plays from YouTube Music or SoundCloud, the recording picked the first time (on any of your devices) is kept in your ProjectM account, and your iPhone and PCs all play that one. "Wrong version? Find it again" replaces it on all your devices.

Same in ProjectM 1.2.3 on Windows.
